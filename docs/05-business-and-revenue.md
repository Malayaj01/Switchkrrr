# Switchkrr Business And Revenue

## Initial Strategy

Keep Switchkrr free at the start. The goal is to validate whether candidates and mentors actually use the Hustle workflow regularly.

Initial focus:
- Small beta.
- One or two job categories, such as APM, product, software, or analyst roles.
- Manually onboard good mentors.
- Measure whether candidates apply more consistently and receive more callbacks.

## Free MVP Benefits

- Easier adoption.
- More candidates sign up.
- Easier to recruit mentors.
- Faster learning.
- More data for matching and product improvement.

## Early Growth Strategy

1. Start with a focused niche.
2. Recruit 10-20 credible mentors.
3. Onboard 50-100 candidates.
4. Track real outcomes.
5. Publish anonymized success stories.
6. Add mentor verification.
7. Add premium features only after repeat usage exists.

## Possible Revenue Models Later

### Candidate Premium Subscription

Candidates pay monthly for:
- more mentor requests
- AI resume analysis
- AI job match scoring
- priority mentor matching
- follow-up reminders
- advanced application analytics

Pros:
- Simple SaaS model.
- Predictable revenue.

Cons:
- Job seekers may be price-sensitive.
- Must deliver clear value.

### Mentor Premium Tools

Mentors pay for:
- personal mentor profile
- analytics
- candidate pipeline tools
- scheduling
- templates
- reputation page

Pros:
- Mentors who monetize career help may pay.

Cons:
- Casual mentors may not pay.

### Paid Mentor Sessions

Mentors charge for:
- resume review
- mock interview
- career strategy call
- referral coaching

Switchkrr takes platform fee.

Pros:
- Strong marketplace model.
- Aligns mentor incentive.

Cons:
- Requires trust, payments, dispute handling.

### Success Fee

Candidate or hiring company pays when candidate gets hired.

Pros:
- Strong value alignment.

Cons:
- Hard to track and enforce.
- May create legal and trust complexity.

### Company Hiring Plans

Companies pay to access verified mentor-backed candidates.

Pros:
- Higher willingness to pay.
- B2B revenue can scale.

Cons:
- More sales-heavy.
- Changes product from candidate-first to hiring marketplace.

### Featured Mentor / Featured Candidate

Paid visibility for mentors or candidates.

Pros:
- Easy to implement.

Cons:
- Can harm trust if not transparent.

### AI Add-Ons

Pay-per-use or subscription for:
- resume analysis
- job description fit score
- personalized outreach messages
- interview prep

Pros:
- Clear cost/value connection.

Cons:
- AI features are easy to copy.
- Requires careful privacy handling.

### Referral Marketplace Fee

Mentors/referrers earn rewards; platform takes a fee on paid referrals or successful outcomes.

Pros:
- Directly tied to referrals.

Cons:
- Can become spammy or ethically questionable if not managed.

## Recommended Revenue Path

### Stage 1: Free Beta

Goal:
- Validate candidate and mentor usage.
- Build trust.
- Collect success stories.

### Stage 2: Candidate Premium

Charge for:
- AI resume analysis
- advanced job match scoring
- extra mentor requests
- advanced tracking

Keep core Hustle free with limits.

### Stage 3: Paid Mentor Services

Allow verified mentors to offer paid services:
- mock interviews
- resume review
- career strategy

Take platform fee.

### Stage 4: B2B Hiring

Offer companies access to verified, high-intent candidates with tracked progress and mentor backing.

## Pricing Ideas Later

- Free: 5 Hustles, 10 pending requests, basic tracking.
- Candidate Pro: monthly subscription for AI and advanced tracking.
- Mentor Pro: tools and public profile.
- Paid Sessions: platform fee per transaction.
- Company Plan: monthly or success-based.

## Key Business Metrics

- Signup conversion.
- Profile completion rate.
- Mentor request rate.
- Mentor approval rate.
- Hustles created.
- Leads per Hustle.
- Applications per candidate.
- Callback rate.
- Interview rate.
- Offer rate.
- Weekly active candidates.
- Weekly active mentors.
- Mentor response time.
